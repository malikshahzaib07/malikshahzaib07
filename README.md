## 👋 About Me

I'm **Malik Shahzaib**, a developer and lifelong learner interested in building useful software across platforms. I enjoy exploring artificial intelligence, designing applications, automating workflows, and turning ambitious ideas into practical projects.

- 🤖 **AI & intelligent tools:** exploring AI-powered applications, assistants, and automation.
- 🌐 **Web development:** building modern, responsive experiences and full-stack projects.
- 📱 **Mobile development:** interested in Android and iOS apps.
- 🖥️ **Desktop & systems:** exploring software for Windows, Linux, and other computing environments.
- 🧪 **Experimentation:** learning new languages, frameworks, runtimes, and developer tools.
- 📚 **Growth mindset:** improving through projects, experiments, feedback, and consistent learning.

> My goal is to become a versatile, full-spectrum developer who can understand a problem, choose the right tools, and build a thoughtful solution.

## 🚀 How I Like to Build

3. **Choose the right tools.** Prefer maintainable solutions over unnecessary complexity.
